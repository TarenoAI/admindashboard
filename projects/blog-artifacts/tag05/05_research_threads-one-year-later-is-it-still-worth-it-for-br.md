# Research — Agent 1

## Topic
**Threads One Year Later: Is It Still Worth It for Brands?**

## Search Intent
- Evaluate whether Threads is strategically relevant after initial hype
- Understand brand-fit conditions, not blanket yes/no advice
- Build a practical execution decision model

## Audience Assumptions
- Audience manages lean social teams
- Wants channel ROI clarity
- Needs decision criteria by brand type and content model
- Concerned about effort allocation across channels

## Competitor Patterns
1. Binary conclusions ("dead" vs "must-use")
2. Weak segmentation by brand maturity and category
3. No channel-role framing
4. Missing failure diagnostics for low-performing Threads efforts
5. Overfocus on short-term spikes

## Competitor Gaps
1. Lack of strategic fit framework
2. No trade-off analysis vs other channels
3. Weak implementation blueprint for test period
4. Limited governance on effort creep
5. Missing exit criteria

## Differentiation Angle
Position Threads as a **context channel**: valuable for certain brand archetypes and messaging styles, weak for others. Use a fit-first decision model.

## Risky / Volatile Claim Zones
- hard growth claims
- exact engagement rates
- platform-wide trend assumptions
- absolute ROI statements

## Claim Safety Direction
Use conditional language and scenario-based recommendations.
