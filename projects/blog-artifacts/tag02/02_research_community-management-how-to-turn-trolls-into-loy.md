# Research — Agent 1 (KB-grounded)

## Topic
**Community Management: How to Turn Trolls into Loyal Fans**

## Search intent
- Primary: Understand a practical operating system for community management.
- Secondary: Reduce execution risk while improving quality and consistency.

## Audience assumptions
- Solo creators handling publication and moderation themselves
- Small teams with limited review capacity
- Agencies needing repeatable outcomes across accounts

## Competitor patterns (Top)
- Tactical lists are common, but decision logic is often missing.
- Most guides focus on actions, not on boundaries and escalation paths.
- Teams are told what to do, but not when to stop, defer, or re-route.

## Competitor gaps (Top)
- Weak “when to use / when not to use” criteria
- Limited cross-functional handoff process
- Minimal weekly feedback loops tied to one KPI

## Differentiation angle
A framework-led, policy-safe workflow that connects daily execution with measurable quality improvement instead of reactive tactics.

## Risky/volatile claim zones
- Time-bound platform behavior claims
- Exact percentages without primary sources
- Guarantees related to growth, bans, or revenue

## Output direction
- English only
- Framework + checklist + FAQ required
- No unsupported hard numbers
