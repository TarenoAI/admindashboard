# Product Inserts — Agent 4

## Insert 1 (after CALM framework)
With Tareno Inbox / Comments, teams can route high-friction threads by risk level, assign clear ownership, and preserve response context across shifts. Instead of duplicate replies and missed escalations, each conversation follows a visible workflow with status, intent, and accountability. This improves consistency under pressure and helps moderators respond faster without sacrificing tone quality or policy compliance.

## Insert 2 (after implementation section)
Using Tareno Inbox / Comments, managers can audit escalation decisions in one dashboard and identify where boundaries were too soft, too harsh, or too late. The team can calibrate response standards in weekly reviews using real thread examples, explicit quality criteria, and shared ownership decisions. This closes the loop between moderation decisions, governance quality, and practical operational learning.

## Insert 3 (before FAQ)
Tareno Inbox / Comments keeps thread history, ownership state, and response intent in one operational view, which is critical when multiple responders rotate through the same account. That continuity prevents contradictory replies, reduces avoidable rework, and protects public trust in tense moments. Over time, teams establish a repeatable moderation rhythm that scales sustainably without losing consistency under daily pressure.
