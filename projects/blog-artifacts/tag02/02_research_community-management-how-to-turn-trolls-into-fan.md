# Research

## Topic
**Community Management: How to Turn Trolls into Fans**

## Search Intent
- Primary: Learn practical, modern workflows for community management tips.
- Secondary: Avoid common mistakes and improve consistency.

## Audience
- Solo creators
- Small marketing teams
- Agencies scaling execution

## Core Findings
1. Consistent process outperforms reactive posting.
2. Clear moderation/automation rules reduce risk and chaos.
3. Weekly review loops improve content quality over time.

## Content Gaps to Cover
- What most teams get wrong
- A practical framework
- When to use / when not to use
- A checklist for implementation
