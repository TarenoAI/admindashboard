# Research — Tag 02
## Topic
**Bio-Link Optimization: The First Step in the SEO Funnel**

## Search Intent
- **Primary intent:** Understand how a bio link page can drive qualified search and social traffic into measurable conversions.
- **Audience:** Creators, solopreneurs, small marketing teams.
- **Funnel stage:** Top and middle funnel (discovery → click → lead capture).

## Core Findings
1. A bio link page is often the highest-frequency touchpoint for social audiences; optimization impacts CTR and downstream conversion quality.
2. SEO impact is indirect but meaningful through:
   - Branded search lift
   - Better internal linking paths
   - Stronger topical relevance signals from consistent messaging
3. The best performing structures prioritize:
   - One clear primary CTA
   - 3–5 contextual secondary links
   - Fast loading and mobile-first layout
   - UTM structure for source-level attribution
4. Common failure mode: link clutter with no hierarchy.

## Recommended Framework
**BLF Framework (Bio-Link Funnel):**
- **B**rief promise above the fold
- **L**ead path as primary CTA
- **F**ocus links ranked by intent and freshness
