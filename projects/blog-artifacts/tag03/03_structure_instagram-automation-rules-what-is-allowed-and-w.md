# Instagram Automation Rules: What Is Allowed and What Gets You Restricted

## TL;DR
- [CORE] Instagram automation is safest when it supports publishing workflow, not fake engagement behavior.
- [CORE] Risk drops when ownership, escalation rules, and weekly review are explicit.
- [DIFF] The SAFE Loop framework helps teams automate consistently without crossing policy boundaries.
- [GAP] Most teams fail by automating volume before defining governance.

## Quick Definition
Instagram automation means using tools to plan, schedule, and manage publishing workflows. It becomes risky when automation imitates human engagement patterns or bypasses platform intent.

## Named Framework: SAFE Loop
- **S — Scope**: define which actions are allowed to automate
- **A — Accountability**: assign owner and approver per workflow step
- **F — Friction Checks**: add stop points before risky actions
- **E — Evaluation**: run weekly compliance and performance review

## When to Use / When Not to Use
### Use
- Content planning and scheduling
- Workflow status, approvals, and handoffs
- Media preparation and draft consistency

### Do not use
- Artificial engagement behavior
- High-frequency action bursts designed to mimic growth
- Any workflow that removes human oversight from risky actions

## Comparison Checklist
| Approach | Policy Risk | Operational Reliability | Scalability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Manual-only | Medium | Low | Low |
| Safe workflow automation | Low-to-moderate | High | High |
| Aggressive engagement automation | High | Unstable | Fragile |
