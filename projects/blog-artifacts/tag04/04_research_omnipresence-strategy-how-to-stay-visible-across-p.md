# Research — Agent 1

## Topic
**Omnipresence Strategy: How to Stay Visible Across Platforms with Repurposing**

## Search intent
- Build sustainable multi-platform visibility without content fatigue
- Learn how to repurpose with quality, not duplication
- Implement a governance model for consistent execution

## Audience assumptions
- Small teams or solopreneurs operating across multiple channels
- Limited production capacity
- Need for operational clarity and measurable improvement

## Competitor patterns
- "Post everywhere" advice without channel-role logic
- Repetitive frameworks with little decision depth
- Minimal governance guidance
- Weak trade-off analysis

## Competitor gaps
- Missing strategic role model per channel
- Missing counterarguments and edge-case handling
- Weak implementation blueprints
- Weak quality control process

## Differentiation angle
Define omnipresence as selective, role-based distribution. Use one core idea, adapted by decision intent per platform, with weekly governance.

## Risky/volatile zones
- Absolute growth promises
- Exact performance percentages without sources
- Time-bound platform behavior claims

## Mode recommendation
- `article_type: authority`
- Reason: Core strategic topic requiring trade-offs, counterarguments, edge cases, and system-level guidance.
