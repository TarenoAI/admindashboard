# Omnipresence Strategy: How to Stay Visible Across Platforms with Repurposing

## TL;DR
- [CORE] Omnipresence is strategic distribution, not mass duplication.
- [DIFF] The ROLE Matrix turns repurposing into a controlled operating model.
- [GAP] Most teams fail from adaptation drift, not lack of ideas.
- [CORE] Governance determines whether scale compounds quality or noise.

## Quick definition
Omnipresence strategy is a role-based system for staying visible across selected platforms by adapting one core message to different decision contexts.

## Named framework: ROLE Matrix
- R = Role of each platform
- O = Original core asset
- L = Level of adaptation by intent
- E = Evaluation cadence and governance

## When to use / when not
### Use
- multi-channel brand building
- limited production capacity
- need for repeatable visibility

### Not
- unclear positioning
- no quality standards
- output-first behavior without adaptation controls

## Comparison table
Full cross-posting vs cosmetic adaptation vs strategic repurposing
