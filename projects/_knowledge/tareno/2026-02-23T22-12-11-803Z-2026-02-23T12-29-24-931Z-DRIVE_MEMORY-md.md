### 2026-02-18 | Agent Pipeline 2.0
**Decision:** 7-Agent system with hard validation

- Agent 2 (Architect): Outlines with word targets per section

- Agent 7 (Publisher): Upload to Drive (review mode) or Live publish

### 2026-02-18 | Publishing Workflow
**Decision:** Two-stage publishing
1. **Review Mode (default):**
   - Save as .md
   - Upload to Google Drive "Tareno Blogs"
   - Notify user: "Ready for approval"
   
2. **Live Mode (with approval):**
   - Run: `npm run blog:publish -- --file "filename.md" --api-base "https://tareno.co"`
   - Backup to Drive

### 2026-02-18 | Quality Standards
**Mandatory per article:**
- 2000+ words (Cluster) / 3500+ words (Pillar)
- TL;DR box at top
- Definition (Agentic format: "X is Y because Z")
- Named Framework
- Comparison Table
- Step-by-Step section
- FAQ (5-8 questions)
- Author bio with concrete credentials (not generic)
- "Last updated" field
- JSON-LD schema (Article, FAQPage, HowTo)
