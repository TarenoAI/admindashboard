# 30-Tage Redaktionsplan Tareno.co
*Mit Subagent-Orchestrierung & Workflow-Zuweisung*

## Übersicht Subagenten (6-Stufen-Pipeline)
| Schritt | Subagent | Aufgabe | Output |
|---------|----------|---------|--------|
| 1 | Research Synthesizer | Faktenbasis, Keywords, Konkurrenz | `01_research_{tag}.md` |
| 2 | SEO & Outline Architect | Struktur, GEO-Blöcke | `02_outline_{tag}.md` |
| 3 | Longform Writer | Abschnittsweise Drafts | `03_section_{tag}_XX.md` |
| 4 | Product-Native Integration | Feature-Inserts | `04_product_{tag}.md` |
| 5 | Editor & E-E-A-T | Redigieren, Claim-Hygiene | `05_edited_{tag}.md` |
| 6 | Entity/Linkability Architect | GEO-Polish, Snippets | `06_final_{tag}.md` |

## Ablauf pro Blog-Tag
```
Tag X: Research → Outline → Writing → Product → Editing → Final
         ↓           ↓          ↓         ↓         ↓        ↓
     Agent 1     Agent 2    Agent 3   Agent 4   Agent 5   Agent 6
```
